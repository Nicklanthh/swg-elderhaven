Welcome to the Haven!Server Features

These are the systems that define Elder Haven. Elder Haven is built like an older RPG first and an MMO checklist second. Progression is meant to come from playing the world, discovering systems and committing to long character paths instead of simply replacing gear every few hours.

◆

Insight / Prestige

Combat, quests, harvesting, survival and bounty work feed one long-term progression system. Invest earned choices into your character, reach the current ceiling, prestige and begin a new cycle with more room to grow.

▣

Retro-RPG faction stories

Custom questlines are closer in spirit to Morrowind-style faction progression than disposable MMO tasks. You meet recurring characters, build your place inside a group and can eventually become part of its leadership, such as becoming leader of the Mandalorians or a Lord or Lady of the Underworld.

▧

Adaptive Weapons & Weaponsmith Rework

Adaptive weapons grow through combat up to Level 5000 and generate tiered crafting materials as they develop. Weaponsmiths can use those fragments and cores to push crafted weapons far beyond their normal limits.

╱

Classic Jedi, less waiting

The familiar Pre-CU Jedi progression remains recognizable, but long artificial delays and Village phase waiting are removed so the path moves at the player's pace.

⚒

Quality-of-life without flattening the game

Faster global progression, multiple terminal missions at once and always-available shuttles/starports reduce dead time while keeping the older SWG world, professions and economy relevant.
