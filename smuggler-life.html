A Smuggler's LifeHome
Become a Lord or Lady of the Underworld. Start as a Master Smuggler looking for work and build your way into the criminal underworld. Run contraband, traffic spice, make dangerous deals and grow a network of contacts across the galaxy. Not every deal goes well.

A Smuggler's Life is a custom Smuggler progression path built around criminal contacts, SpyNet, spice trafficking, negotiation, intimidation and the less respectable side of the galaxy. The long-term goal is to rise from experienced smuggler to Lord or Lady of the Underworld, gaining access to your own criminal network and Smuggler Runs.

Requirement

You must be a Master Smuggler to begin the questline.

Where to start

Travel to Coronet, Corellia and find Loquito Quickshoot, a seasoned Rodian Smuggler waiting outside the Coronet Spaceport. Speak with him to begin A Smuggler's Life.

What to expect

Quickshoot introduces you to SpyNet and a network of spice dealers, fixers, criminals and other contacts. Expect Persuade, Intimidate, shady favors and deals where the other side may decide that talking is over. Some contacts can be convinced. Some can be threatened. Sometimes bringing a blaster is simply better business.

Smuggler Runs

Move product. Find the buyer. Try not to get shot. Smuggler Runs are repeatable underworld jobs built around travel, contraband and risk rather than a mission-terminal loop.

A run starts through your criminal contacts on Corellia. You meet a provider, acquire the shipment and get told where the deal is going down. From there you travel to the destination, track down the buyer, complete the exchange and eventually report back to Zapaya.

The trip is part of the job. Buyers are not standing directly beside the starport, directions matter, and not every run stays clean. A route can attract opportunists, a deal can become dangerous and sometimes the safest-looking delivery is the one that goes sideways.

Smuggler Runs are being tuned to keep that tension without wasting the player's time: contacts remain out in the world, but routing and directions are being tightened so the challenge comes from the run itself rather than pointless hiking.

The endgame fantasy

This path is not meant to end with a badge and a thank-you screen. The goal is to climb through the underworld until your character becomes a Lord or Lady of the Underworld, with a criminal network and repeatable operations that continue beyond the main story.

Development status: A Smuggler's Life and Smuggler Runs are still being actively built. Quest content, rewards, routes and balance may change before the alpha release.
