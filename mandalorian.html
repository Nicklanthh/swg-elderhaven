Mandalorian PathHome
Become the leader of the Mandalorians. Earn your place piece by piece, build a suit of armor that grows with you, master exclusive Mandalorian technology and eventually take command of the Mandalorian Enclave.

The Mandalorian Path is a custom progression route built around the questline, Adaptive Mandalorian Armor, exclusive Fuel-powered abilities, and ultimately leadership of the Mandalorians.

Starting the Path

To begin, you must be a Master Bounty Hunter and speak with Peshisho in Mos Eisley. Completing the questline rewards a full set of Adaptive Mandalorian Armor.

Adaptive Mandalorian Armor

The armor begins at only 1% on all stats and grows stronger through use. As it levels, it gains substantially higher stats, including high lightsaber resistance. Regular Mandalorian Armor can still be crafted normally, but the Adaptive version is exclusive to the Mandalorian Path.

Fuel System

Mandalorians unlock two exclusive abilities: the Personal Shield Generator and the Micro Missile. Both consume Fuel.

Fuel works as a permanent extra resource bar with a maximum capacity of 100. To regenerate it, you must carry a Fuel resource in your inventory. Any Fuel resource type works. Fuel currently regenerates at 1 unit per second.

Mandalorian Enclave

Completing the path grants control of the Mandalorian Enclave. As its leader, you can dispatch your Mandalorians on missions for different rewards. These missions are currently available every 12 hours.

The 12-hour mission interval and other balance values may be adjusted based on player feedback.
