Jedi UnlockHome

The goal is to keep the classic Jedi progression while removing unnecessary waiting.

Requirements

  • 7 Master professions — any professions you want.
  • 5 exploration badges.
  • 1 themepark.

Once the requirements are complete, the Old Man appears within 5–10 seconds and will continue to appear until you accept his crystal. There is no hours-long waiting period. The attacking Sith appear 30 seconds after you pick up the crystal, so be prepared.

At the Village, you need 24 Force Sensitive boxes before fighting Mellichae and becoming eligible for the Padawan Trials. There are no Village phases and no quests required to unlock individual Force Sensitive branches. There are also no Village phase delays, so you can progress at your own pace.

Padawan and Jedi Knight Trials remain mostly traditional, although they may be adjusted based on player feedback.

Important: Unlocking Jedi requires giving up your previous templates. Jedi use custom modifiers that make them extremely powerful on their own.

Planned: a custom quest will eventually replace the themepark requirement.
